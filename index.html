      Questions

      The short answers.

      Which EasyWorship versions does it work with?

      It creates EasyWorship 7 schedules (.ewsx), using the same format and theme settings as a schedule saved from EasyWorship 7.

      Does the AI change our lyrics?

      No. The AI only decides where songs start and end, the titles, and the verse and chorus labels. The words on the slides come straight from your document. The only automatic tidy-ups are spacing, removing markers like “x3”, and capitalising the first letter of each line.

      What if a song isn't found by search?

      Add it with “+ Song” and paste the lyrics, or include it in your .docx. If a song is found but its lyrics aren't, it's added with its title so you can paste the words in.

      Is my document uploaded anywhere?

      With AI clean-up on, the document's text is sent to Groq and Google Gemini to sort it. Turn AI clean-up off to process everything in your browser with the basic rules. The schedule file itself is always built in your browser.

      Do we need a licence to project songs?

      Yes. Projecting copyrighted songs normally needs a licence such as CCLI. This tool helps you prepare slides; it doesn't grant any rights to the songs.
