Lyrics → EasyWorship

Upload a Word document of song lyrics and get an EasyWorship schedule (.ewsx) with every song split into slides. Check the songs below, fix anything that came out wrong, then download.

A new song starts at each number (“2.”), each “Lyrics: Title by Artist” line, or each ALL-CAPS title. Blank lines split verses, and labels like [Chorus] are left out. In the lyrics boxes, a blank line always starts a new slide.
