Lyrics → EasyWorship

Upload a Word document of song lyrics in any layout, search for songs to add, or both. AI sorts it into title slides and songs, marks verses, choruses and bridges, and tidies the text. You can then reorder the schedule, preview every slide and download a .ewsx for EasyWorship.

Find a song

    Lyrics come from LRCLIB and the web. Make sure your church is licensed (e.g. CCLI) to project the songs you use.

      Drag items in the schedule to reorder them. In the lyrics boxes, a [Chorus] line names the part below it, and a blank line starts a new slide.
